## App Description (4000 characters max)

**Digitl** - The Ultimate Number Puzzle Challenge

Transform numbers into solutions in this elegantly designed puzzle game that combines mathematical precision with addictive gameplay.

**How to Play**
Use the given digits and four basic operations (+, -, ×, ÷) to reach the target number. Tap digits to select them, choose an operation, and watch as your calculations unfold. Each puzzle has a unique solution waiting to be discovered.

**Features**
• **Three Difficulty Levels** - Start with Easy (4 digits), progress to Medium (5 digits), and master Hard (6 digits)
• **250+ Unique Puzzles** per difficulty level - Hundreds of carefully crafted challenges
• **Clean, Retro Design** - Sleek calculator-inspired interface with digital display aesthetics
• **Smooth Gameplay** - Intuitive controls and satisfying animations
• **Track Your Progress** - Complete puzzles and unlock new challenges
• **Daily Challenges** - Fresh puzzles to keep you engaged

**Perfect For**
• Math enthusiasts looking for a mental workout
• Puzzle lovers seeking a fresh challenge
• Anyone who enjoys strategic thinking games
• Students wanting to improve mental math skills

**Challenge Your Mind**
Each puzzle requires logical thinking, pattern recognition, and mathematical skill. Start simple and work your way up to increasingly complex challenges that will test your problem-solving abilities.
